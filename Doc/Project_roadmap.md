\## 🗺️ SQL Data Analytics Project Roadmap



This project roadmap outlines a structured approach to applying SQL for real-world \*\*Data Analytics\*\* tasks. Designed by \*\*Sumit Kumar Soni\*\*, this collection of analytics mini-projects builds analytical thinking, business acumen, and SQL proficiency.



\---



\### 🔢 10-Stage SQL Analytics Roadmap



1\. Pivot to "Discount Margin" Or "Discount Tracking"



2\. Seasonal trends for top three items



3\. Reps consistently missing quarterly quotas



4\. The VIP Client List: Who are our top 5 customers by total revenue?



5\. Deal Size Breakdown: How much revenue comes from Small, Medium, and Large deals



6\. Fulfillment Risk: What is the order cancellation rate by Country



7\. Product Profitability: Which product lines generate the highest average revenue per item?



8\. Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth: How are total sales trending by Year and Quarter?



9\. Discount Abuse: Which specific products are being sold below MSRP most often?



10\. Customer Frequency: Which customers order from us the most often?
